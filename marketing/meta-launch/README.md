## Ad roles

- **Ad A — curiosity test.** Scroll-stopper: `$1 → $5,000,000?` read in one
  second; the impossible number is the hook.
- **Ad B — participation test.** Direct response: challenges the viewer —
  `SOMETHING BETTER?` is the punch, `▶ TRADE #1 OFFERS ARE OPEN` the urgency.

**Recommendation:** run Ad A as the curiosity arm and Ad B as the
participation arm of the same ad test; same audience, same budget, compare
CTR + landing actions.
